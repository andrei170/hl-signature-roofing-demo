Built for the Willamette Valley

Rain and moss are what wear a roof out here

Salem roofs spend most of fall, winter and spring wet. Moss holds that water against the shingles, lifts their edges and lets water work its way under. Most leaks we are called to start small, at flashing, vents, valleys and around chimneys.

  • Moss and debris checked on every visit
  • Flashing, vents and pipe boots sealed properly
  • Gutters and valleys checked so water leaves the roof
  • Photos of everything we find, so you see what we see
